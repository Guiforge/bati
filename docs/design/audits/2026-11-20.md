# UX audit, 2026-11-20

The whole app this time, sub-menus included, judged three ways: by screen (144 captures, English
and French, at the AVD's real 420 dpi), by journey (12 goals given to agents as goals, never as
paths, on the emulator), and by pattern (a consistency inspector over the code). Four personas
then read the captures: the stranger, the hurried lifter, the player, the regular. The scout's
proposals are in §8. The plan and its phase log are in [2026-11-20-plan.md](2026-11-20-plan.md).

Every finding below was checked against the picture and, where it makes a claim about behaviour,
against the code or a replay on the emulator. §5 lists what did not survive that.

Evidence: `.audit/` (gitignored). Captures `.audit/{en,fr}/{audit,audit-more,audit-fresh}/`,
journeys `.audit/flows/<task>/`, persona reports `.audit/judgement/`.

## 2. Confirmed bugs

1. **"Next reminder: 8 Tuesday at 4:45 PM".** `weekdayLabel`
   (`components/settings/ReminderSection.tsx:121`) formats `{ weekday: "long", day: "numeric" }`
   once the date is more than six days out (after a pause), and English `Intl` renders that
   "8 Tuesday". French gives "mardi 8". Seen in `.audit/flows/knee/01-settings.png`. Fix: add
   `month: "short"` so English reads "Tuesday, Dec 8", or build the phrase from two formats.

2. **Health Connect sync runs before the migrations on a fresh install.** An `AppState` change
   fires `syncHealthConnectQuietly` (`src/healthExport/inStep.ts:52`), which reads a preference
   before `user_preferences` exists: `[healthExport.sync] no such table: user_preferences`, shown
   as an error toast in dev. Found while capturing, 2026-11-20. Harmless today (the sync retries),
   but it writes an error to the hero's log on every first launch. Fix: wait for the database to be
   ready, as the rest of the app does.
3. **The first "Start" tap of onboarding is sometimes lost.** *(Not seen again after 2.: four fresh
   installs in a row passed on merged main, 2026-11-21, with the dev error toast gone. The leading
   hypothesis is that the toast took the tap; a double redirect on cold start is the other one.
   Watch it, do not close it.)* On a cold fresh install the
   presentation's "Start my training adventure" (`router.push`) does nothing about one time in two
   under Maestro, on main and on branches alike; a second tap works. Found while capturing. Looks
   like the cold-start lost tap already noted for the village; not reproduced by hand reliably,
   not fixed. The capture flows of the redesign branches retry the tap locally.

No other bug survived verification. Three that looked like bugs are in §5.

## 3. Friction

Ranked by how many people it costs. "Seen by" names the journeys and personas that raised it.

**Finding things**

- **F1. Rare, important features live in one long Settings list.** Reminder, break, spared zone,
  set-aside exercises and backup are all rows of one scroll, Backup about three screens down
  under Reminder. The "Android isn't backing up Bati" card on Home opens Settings at the top,
  not at Backup. Seen by: journeys 1, 3, 4, 9; scout. Fix: the card scrolls to Backup; the larger
  answer is §6.
- **F2. Two "Pause" in Settings that do different things.** "Pause 1 wk / 2 wk" under Reminder
  silences the reminder; "Pause and zone" is the holiday break that protects the flame, and its
  name does not say so. Journey 3 found it by elimination. Fix: rename the reminder one "Mute
  reminders" and the row something that says what it keeps ("Break and rest zone" or better).
- **F3. Set-aside has no duration and does not point to the zone.** A knee that hurts "for a
  while" is "Pause and zone", but the hero goes to "Set-aside exercises" first, which is
  per-exercise and permanent (`.audit/flows/knee/02-set-aside.png`). Fix: one line on the empty
  set-aside screen linking to "Pause and zone" for a limited time.
- **F4. The exercise library is an unlabeled dumbbell icon beside "Create" on Quests,** and
  "Create" on the library opens the Forge hub rather than the exercise form. Journey 2 guessed.

**Doing things**

- **F5. Settings rows give no clue what a tap does.** A row that cycles (Language, Equipment), one
  that toggles (Haptics), one that opens a screen (Outing display) and one that opens a sheet
  (My hero, safe) all look the same: `SettingRow` draws no chevron and no switch
  (`components/settings/SettingRow.tsx`). The sharpest case: **Language cycles a ring of seven**
  (`cycleLanguage` -> `nextAppLanguage`, `app/settings.tsx:471`), so French then back to English
  went through German and Chinese (`.audit/flows/language-village/03-lang-back.png`). Fix: a
  chevron on navigating rows, a switch on toggles, a list for Language.
- **F6. The break counts in whole weeks.** 1 to 8 chips; a 10-day holiday becomes 14
  (`.audit/flows/vacation/02-pause-zone.png`).
- **F7. Zones are muscle groups, not joints.** A knee means "Legs", which removes every leg
  exercise (`.audit/flows/knee/05-spared.png`). Product question more than a fix.
- **F8. A hero's exercise named like a built-in one sits two rows from it** ("Wall sit" Yours,
  "Wall Sit" Legs) with no warning at creation; and a hold exercise lands in a quest as "Reps 10"
  until someone notices the Seconds toggle (`.audit/flows/own-exercise/09`, `11`).
- **F9. The adventure editor says "at least two steps" only after Save,** below the fold
  (`.audit/flows/share-adventure/08-saved.png`).
- **F10. The Journal tab reopens the last detail screen** instead of the Journal
  (`.audit/flows/squat-record/01-journal.png`); the audit flow needed a double tap on the tab to
  get back to its root. Fix: reset the tab's stack when it is tapped while a detail is on top.
- **F11. Withdrawn.** "A past set's number is typeable, but only − and + are drawn": `CountInput`
  draws an underline for exactly this ("The underline is the whole affordance"); the journey ran
  at 240 dpi, where the line is a hair.

- **F20. Quick actions: the right idea, badly carried out** (added at Guillaume's request after
  the journeys). Starting a walk, a run or a ride straight from Home is the shortcut the hurried
  lifter asks for, and the tap does start the outing at once (`startOuting`,
  `components/home/QuickActions.tsx:282`). What lets it down, in `audit/01-home-top.png` and
  `audit-fresh/45-home-day-one.png`:
  - The tiles say "Warden's Walk", "Messenger's Run", "Outrider's Ride": nothing plain says walk,
    run or bike, there is no icon, and the art is three dark scenes that read alike.
  - Each tile carries two targets: the tile starts, the "45 min" chip opens the goal sheet. The
    chip looks like a label, and on day one it is missing, so the goal is invisible.
  - Choosing the tiles is a hidden gesture: "Long-press to set", in 11 sp grey.
  - Three tiles on a row built for more leave a hole on the right.
  - Nothing says the tap starts right away, with GPS: the hero learns it from the session screen.
  Redesign candidate (§9, 5).

**Understanding things (the stranger)**

- **F12. Day one speaks game and gym before the first session.** "Tier 1", "Circuit", "Quick
  actions / Long-press to set" on Home day one (`audit-fresh/45`); "rounds" and five exercise
  names nobody knows on the first-session card (`audit-fresh/44`); "reps" and "sets" never
  defined, including on the quit dialog, where the safe button says "your sets stay"
  (`audit-more/82`). Fix: hide Tier and Quick actions until the first session; say "5 moves, done
  twice"; drop "sets" from the quit dialog.
- **F13. The first session starts with a 3-minute warm-up the hero did not choose.** The card said
  "≈ 10 min"; the next screen is "WARM-UP 1/5, 3:10 left" (`.audit/flows/first-session/05`).
  "Skip warm-up" is easy to find, but the hero briefly took the warm-up for the workout. Fix:
  "+ 3 min warm-up" on the card.
- **F14. Adventures and Quests both read as "start here"** on day one, explained in RPG words
  (`audit-fresh/47`, `48`). Fix: one line under each tab title, "a plan over weeks" vs "one
  workout".
- **F15. Village and Journal on day one have nothing to do.** A dark hut and "Something watches
  Testville." (`audit-fresh/49`); a page of zeros (`audit-fresh/50`). Neither offers the first
  session.

**Feeling it (the player and the regular)**

- **F16. Victory pays out a number.** "+44 XP", a thin bar, "Total Time 2:16"; the village event
  sits under the fold, and the comparison the journal already computes ("#10 of 15… 5 under your
  best", `audit/22b`) is absent (`audit/33-victory.png`). Raised by both the player and the
  regular, for opposite reasons: the player wants the moment, the regular the comparison. One
  screen answers both. Redesign candidate.
- **F17. One exercise's progress over time is nowhere.** The exercise page has "Last time" and
  "Record", nothing between (`audit/12-exercise-detail.png`); 62 standing records with no list
  (`audit-more/66`); a week of hard sets with no last week to compare (`audit/19`). Redesign
  candidate.
- **F18. The rest screen of a boss fight shows no boss.** The arena is there during the exercise
  (`fr/audit/36-boss.png`), gone during the rest, where the hero spends half the fight
  (`en/audit/36-boss.png`).
- **F19. The village is read as a list of ranks** ("Quarry rank 9", "Rank 10 in about 409 back
  sessions", `audit/15`, `16`), the second phrase demoralising for the regular.

## 4. Polish

- The day row is disabled while the reminder is off (on purpose), but only dimmed: a tap says
  nothing (`.audit/flows/reminder/05`).
- Three back controls (bare chevron, 44 dp disc, 36 dp journal disc) and two title sizes; and
  `quest-import.tsx:180`, `pack-share.tsx:178` hand-copy `ScreenBackButton`.
- Delete drawn four ways: red trash icon (quest editor), red text (boss editor), neutral outline
  (exercise, the only one not red), red outline (journal).
- Dismiss X on the Update and Sync Home cards is 28 dp with no hitSlop, beside 44 dp siblings
  (`UpdateCard.tsx:70`, `SyncCard.tsx:177`).
- Two button families: `NButton` (gold, 40 dp) in the Journal, `AppButton` (orange, 44 dp)
  elsewhere, for the same role.
- Four editors re-declare `DialogState` instead of `useConfirmDialog`.
- "Send to another app" in Settings reads as a caption, not a link.
- The rest screen's −/+ look small under a huge timer (36 dp drawn, 52 dp with hitSlop: the
  target is fine, the weight is not). "How to do it / Replace / I couldn't do this one" are three
  small grey text links.
- The quit dialog puts the red "Quit quest" first and Cancel last.
- Most controls are 44 dp; Android's guideline is 48. Decide the floor once.
- "+" in the adventure quest picker gives no feedback; the step placeholder repeats on each step.
- Village name commits only on the keyboard key; a half-translated frame for ~1.5 s after a
  language switch.
- "Discard" on Home's resume card throws a session away with no confirmation (journey 9 setup);
  check whether that is intended.

## 5. Read wrong, or not the app

- **The session pause button "too small".** I raised it myself from 54x54 px; the AVD had its
  density forced to 240, so that is 36 dp plus hitSlop 8, 52 dp. It worked on the first tap in
  journey 7.
- **"Tapping the avatar / the backup card does nothing"** (journey 9). Replayed: both open
  Settings (`home-settings` is the avatar, the gear badge has `pointerEvents="none"`; the card
  has `onPress`). The agent tapped during the transition after "Discard".
- **"0 / 28 achievements" beside a full 100/100 bar** (player, regular). The seed writes history
  without `checkForNewAchievements`, which unlocks every reached threshold (`>=`) on the next
  save (`db/achievements.ts:505`); that is why the victory screen then awards "First Steps". A
  real hero only meets it after a restore, until the next session.
- **The village tab as an empty skeleton** (player). Capture timing: the French run shows the
  village drawn.
- **"The adventure promises a boss and ends without one"** (player). "The Squire's Path" is a
  route; routes end without a boss by design.
- **"A quest of your own" with no repeat button** (journey 5). A seed row (`__dev_xp`).
- **All seven days ticked and 4:45 PM when the reminder is switched on** (journey 1).
  `suggestDays` / `suggestTime` read the history, and the seeded hero trains daily.
- Journeys 1 to 8 ran at the forced 240 dpi: their tap counts hold, nothing they say about what
  fits on a screen does. All captures were retaken at 420 dpi.

## 6. Shown or searched: the grid

The question behind this audit: what to put in front of the hero, what to let them find.

| | Often | Rarely |
|---|---|---|
| **Matters** | on the main path: start a session, log a set, see the result | **offered at the moment it matters** |
| **Minor** | one tap | Settings |

Where today's features sit, and where the journeys say they belong:

| Feature | Today | Belongs | Trigger that would offer it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reminder | Settings, under Reminder | rare, matters | after the 3rd session, or the first missed planned day |
| Break (holiday) | Settings, "Pause and zone" | rare, matters | when the flame is about to go out after days of silence; when the hero sets an oath end date |
| Spared zone | same screen | rare, matters | "I'm stopping, it hurts" in a session (already a door) and on the empty set-aside screen |
| Backup | Settings, three screens down | rare, matters | the Home card (exists), landing on Backup instead of the top |
| Own exercise | Quests > dumbbell > Create > Forge | rare, minor | fine where it is, once the dumbbell has a label |
| Language | Settings | rare, minor | Settings, as a list |

The rare-and-important column is not solved by another button on Home. It is solved by the
moment: the app already knows when a reminder, a break or a spared zone would help, and the
journeys show that the hero does not know these exist until that moment.

## 7. Where the personas disagree

- **Victory (F16).** The player wants the village and the level bar to be the show; the regular
  wants the comparison with the last time. The lifter wants Continue to stay pinned and one tap
  away. All three fit one screen; none fits the current one.
- **Warm-up (F13).** The lifter wants it skippable by default; the stranger needs it explained,
  not removed. Answer for the stranger first: the warm-up stays, the card says it is there.
- **Day-one vocabulary (F12).** The stranger asks to hide Tier, Quick actions and the shelf until
  the first session; the player wants the game visible from the first screen. Hiding for one
  session costs the player nothing they can use yet.

## 8. The scout

Full text in `.audit/judgement/scout.md`. It skipped what [references.md](references.md) already
holds. Ranked by value for cost:

1. **Reveal on earn** (small, F12, F15). Until the first session is done, hide Tier, Quick
   actions, records, "lit" and the shelf; gloss each term once, at its first use, with a per-term
   flag. No glossary screen.
2. **Grouped Settings with a kind per row** (small, F1, F5). Five headers in the existing list
   (Training, Reminders and breaks, Your hero, Display, About) and a `kind` on `SettingRow` that
   draws a chevron or a switch.
3. **Ask for the reminder after the first win** (small, §6). A card on the victory of session 1
   (and 3 if still unset): "Train again Thursday?", two preset chips, "Not now". It writes the same
   reminder Settings does.
4. **A sparkline per exercise** (small, F17). Hevy's exercise page has a progress chart and a
   history list ([Hevy help](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/35382889578135)). Here:
   the last 12 sessions under "Your numbers", the record ringed, a tap to the list.
   `getExerciseHistory` (`db/personalRecords.ts:120`) already returns the data.
5. **A records list** (small, F17). "Records standing 62" opens movement, record, date, last time,
   with a dot on this week's ([Hevy help](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/35649367857175)).
6. **One beat per tap on victory** (medium, F16). Duolingo shows one event per Continue and keeps
   the big animation for milestones ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/streak-milestone-design-animation)).
   Here: open on the one headline (village event, else level-up, else the XP bar filling), then
   the stats, then only what is new; the particle burst only on a tier, a level or a boss kill.
7. **"It hurts" leads to the zone** (medium, F3, F7, §6). Fitbod has one Injuries entry with a
   one-tap "Mark as not injured" ([Fitbod help](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/articles/37629269518103)).
   Here: "I'm stopping, it hurts" offers "Spare this zone?" with a 1 or 2 week auto-lift, an active
   zone shows as one line on Home, and set-aside and Pause and zone become one screen.

Several of the scout's claims about other apps are marked unverified in its file; the four
linked above are sourced.

## 9. Proposed triage

**Bug, straight to a fix:** §2.1.

**Fix, one small change each (phase 5, in batches):**
- *Finding:* F1 (card lands on Backup), F2 (two names), F3 (link from set-aside), F4 (label the
  dumbbell), F10 (Journal tab resets).
- *Settings:* F5 (chevrons, switches, a Language list), F6 (days, not weeks) if the product
  agrees.
- *Editors:* F8 (duplicate-name warning, reps/seconds at creation), F9 (say "two steps" up front),
  F11 (a pencil on the set number).
- *Day one:* F12, F13, F14, F15 (copy and hiding, no new screen).
- *Patterns:* the §4 list, as one consistency batch (back control, delete, dismiss X,
  `useConfirmDialog`).

**Redesign candidates (choose at most three):**
1. **Victory** (F16, F18): the moment and the comparison on one screen.
2. **Exercise progress** (F17): a history per exercise, reachable from the records.
3. **The moments that offer rare features** (§6): reminder, break, spared zone, offered when they
   matter instead of in Settings.
4. **Settings structure** (F1, F5): sections that open, instead of one list.
5. **Quick actions** (F20): plain names and an icon per kind (walk, run, ride), the goal visible on
   every tile, the setting reachable without a long-press, and a word that the tap starts now.
   Added after the choice of 2026-11-20 was made; not in it.

## 12. Phase 6, redesigns (2026-11-21)

actions), judged the before/after on a review page and approved all four "after fixes". Each was
built on its own branch in a worktree, captured at 420 dpi, judged by the four personas, fixed,

| Merge | Answers |

| `redesign/exercise-progress` | F17: a 12-session curve on the exercise page, every record in one list |
| `redesign/quick-actions` | F20: Walk, Run, Ride with an icon and the world's name under it, the goal on every tile |
| `fix/audit-bugs` | §2.2: Health Connect sync waits for the migrations |

Then the persona audit of the skill (`audit-personas-bati`) passed them with corrections, no red
line: "lowest of N" removed, French "marque" for a session's best, one filled button on the
painful-stop screen, one word for rest, the privacy policy updated (`fbc5bc6a6`).

Phase 5 on main besides: Settings rows draw a chevron or a switch and Language opens a list, the
break counts in days, a warning when an exercise name is taken, no "Tier 1" before the first
session.

Final pass on merged main: `audit`, `audit-more`, `audit-timely`, `audit-fresh`, 78 captures in
`.audit/final/`, full suite green (457 suites).

batch, the break offer seen only in a test (it needs seven days of silence), bug §2.3 to watch,
