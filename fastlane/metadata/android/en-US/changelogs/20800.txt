- A quest someone sent you opens in Bati with one tap,
  straight from your chat, mail or Files.
- Before it joins yours, rename it and leave out the
  movements you don't want. A quest you already have
  can be updated or kept beside the new one.
- A movement your version doesn't know yet no longer
  blocks the whole quest: it is simply left out.
- Received files are checked more closely: oversized
  pictures and hidden characters stay out.
