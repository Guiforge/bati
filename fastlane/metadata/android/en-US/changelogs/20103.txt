- The warm-up only prescribes movements you can do. Without
  a bar it was still asking for one, on any pulling quest.
- Every warm-up movement counts its last seconds out loud,
  not only the countdown that follows the warm-up.
- Reading what a movement is stops the clock, from the
  running screen, a rest, or the warm-up's "up next" card.
- A swapped movement keeps the reps you set. Trading seconds
  for reps threw every tap away.
