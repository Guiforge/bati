## 💎 What You Earn

Every workout gives you:

| Reward | From | What It Does |
|--------|------|--------------|
| ⭐ **XP** | All workouts | Level up your hero |
| 🪙 **Gold** | All workouts | Universal currency |
| 🔥 **Flame** | Daily streak | Shows dedication |
| 🏆 **Boss Tokens** | Defeating bosses | Legendary buildings |

### Muscle → Resource → Building

| Your Workout | Resource | Builds |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| 💪 Arms | 🪵 Wood | Archery Range → Watchtower |
| 🦴 Back | 🪨 Stone | Quarry → Castle Walls |
| 🫀 Chest | 🔥 Fire | Forge → Armory |
| 🎯 Abs | 💧 Water | Well → Fountain |
| 🦅 Shoulders | 🌬️ Wind | Windmill → Observatory |
| 🦵 Legs | 🌾 Grain | Farm → Barn |

**Your village reflects YOU:**

- Train arms often? → Impressive Archery Range
- Love back day? → Massive Castle Walls
- Balanced training? → Well-rounded kingdom

## 🏰 Your Village

### How It Works

1. **No choices** — Buildings appear automatically
2. **Train more** → Buildings level up (1→5)
3. **Beat bosses** → Unlock legendary buildings

### Building Tiers

| Tier | Examples | Unlock |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Starter** | Campfire, Tent | Default |
| **Basic** | Forge, Farm, Well | 5 sessions |
| **Advanced** | Armory, Castle Wall | Level 10+ |
| **Legendary** | Dragon Lair, Hero's Hall | Boss rewards |

### Building Levels

Each building evolves as you train:

```
Level 1: ⬜ Basic hut
Level 2: ⬛ Improved
Level 3: 🏠 Notable
Level 4: 🏛️ Major
Level 5: 🏰 LEGENDARY
```

## ✨ Design Philosophy

1. **Many small rewards** — Feel progress every session
2. **Visual feedback** — See your village grow
3. **No grinding** — Just train, rewards follow
4. **Personal fingerprint** — Village is uniquely yours
5. **Sport first** — RPG enhances, never distracts
