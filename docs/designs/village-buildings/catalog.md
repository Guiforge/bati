# Bati village: building idea catalog (v4, 02/11/2026)

A menu, not a spec. Guillaume strikes, keeps or swaps any idea before the
buildings track paints it. The planner turns the kept ideas into one card per
building: fewer, bigger, readable ideas beat many small ones.

Today's iso look of a building is not a constraint: nothing was shipped, so a card may keep it, rework it or start over completely. Only the emprise (the village map is built around it) and the ranks, thresholds, XP and unlock rules stay.

Rules from the prompt (sections 6 and 7), in short:
- **Mythic** adds three new things: one towering element + one magic or prestige layer (visible by day AND by night) + the gag finale. Earlier add-ons stay, painted in, smaller and calmer.
- **Growth**: each stage is bigger than the one before (at least +15 % silhouette area or +10 % height) and adds a new element on its silhouette. Never just "taller with nicer materials".
- Only 2 buildings end with statues (the Hall of Heroes, the Observatory's atlantes). Only the Wizard Tower floats. Only the Observatory has rings. No glow on weapons or armour, no coins, no treasure, no gilding, no glint: nothing looks like loot (ochre, brass and bronze painted matte).
- **Night**: the night is a colour grade of the day painting plus code light at anchors. An object that only exists at night (a low moon, an aurora) is the one night-only sprite allowed per stage.
- **Gags**: never a weapon aimed at a person, never a wound. Every [wink] has a fallback beat in case Guillaume strikes it.
- Readability: add-ons at least 10 dp on screen, on the silhouette edge or in the gag slot; at most 2 figures (people or animals) per gag or build gag, each at least 14 dp tall; ground contact inside the footprint mask, everything inside the canvas.
- The clan mark has its own forms per stage; gags never use it (plain ribbons instead).

**Rank rhythm for the 12 buildings with 10 ranks**: ranks 1, 3, 5, 7 and 10 bring a new stage painting and the upgrade show. Ranks 2, 4, 6 and 8 bring the next gag beat AND a site teaser: the start of the next stage's new element on site (a scaffold, a pile of cut stone, a half-raised mast, a crate of brass parts), at least 16 dp, that the next stage paints finished. Rank 9 brings the mythic's site, the biggest scaffold of the ladder, plus one add-on the mythic keeps (a lantern, a banner, an animal, a prop). The finale beat lands at rank 10. Deeds put a teaser at rank 2; upgrades place their beats as listed. (The buildings session also tests a variant where each in-between rank gets its own painting of the stage with the teaser painted in; Guillaume picks.)

**Real-world references** (text only, for the maquette and the words of the prompt, never sent to FLUX as images): the La Mancha windmills, the Carnac stones, the telamons of Agrigento, the treadwheel cranes and scaffolds of Guédelon for every build site, a village forge, a Breton well. The planner adds 2 or 3 per card.

**Each entry**: Hook · Ladder (rough, solid, grand, legendary, mythic) · Running gag (rN = rank N; each beat replaces the last in the gag slot) · Build gag (on the site during the show) · Night · Never drawn. [PD] marks a public-domain source, [wink] a modern nod that Guillaume approves or strikes.

## Muscles (6), ranks 1 to 10 by lifetime reps

### Archery Range (arms)
- **Hook**: straw targets in a row, a firing line, a fence. Ochre and red rings.
- **Ladder**: rough, hay-bale targets and a rope line · solid, painted targets and a shooting shed · grand, a covered gallery and a target sliding on a pulley rope · legendary, a judges' box and a swinging pendulum target · mythic, a tall timber frame stacking three giant targets (towering), red and ochre streamers on the arrows, fluttering by day and lantern-lit at night (layer), the finale.
- **Running gag, the apprentice's aim**: r2 arrows stuck in the fence, the instructor ducking · r4 a hay bale bristling with arrows · r6 a first hit on the target, the apprentice jumps for joy · r8 a bullseye, the target's crow flies off · r10 an arrow split in two in the bullseye [PD, Robin Hood].
- **Optional beat, Guillaume's call** [wink]: at r9, a relaxed retired guard on a bench, an arrow stuck in the thick padding of his knee guard, clearly fine, sipping a drink (never blood, never a limp). It only works for people who know the line, and G-WINK will name the game: approve or strike. **Fallback**: at r9, the instructor hangs a riddled old target on the shed wall like a trophy.
- **Build gag**: an apple balanced on a straw dummy's head, the apprentice aiming with one eye shut, the instructor peeking between his fingers [PD, William Tell].
- **Never drawn**: a green hood with a feather, any film or cartoon outlaw, any game armour or insignia.

### Windmill (shoulders)
- **Hook**: turning sails, a stone tower, flour sacks. White and ochre.
- **Ladder**: rough, a small post mill on a trestle with patched sails · solid, a stone tower mill with full sails and a flour cart · grand, a bigger cap, a gallery balcony, an oven built into the base with smoke · legendary, huge sky-blue sails and a weather vane · mythic, a blue crystal spike as a lightning rod on the cap, at least a quarter of the tower's height, never an orb (towering, it feeds the Observatory), sails that catch visible ribbons of wind by day and glow blue at night (layer), the finale.

- **Running gag, the knight and the giants** [PD, Don Quixote, 1605]: r2 a lanky knight on a skinny horse stares at the mill · r4 he charges with his lance · r6 he hangs from one sail and turns with it (drawn into a rank 6 sail variant), his horse waiting below in the gag slot · r8 he shares bread with the miller · r10 the knight and his squire picnic on the gallery, his lance now the mill's flagpole with a plain ribbon.
- **Shoulders**: the miller carries a sack on each shoulder from grand on.
- **Sails**: a separate sprite turned by the app. One variant per stage: patched, full, big, sky-blue, then the wind ribbons.
- **Build gag**: a flour cloud turns two workers white.
- **Never drawn**: any film, cartoon or famous sketched version of the knight.

## Upgrades (6): ranks 1 to 3, then rule A or B
Each rank of an upgrade is a stage, so each brings a new painting and a gag beat. Under rule A only the first three stages exist. Under rule B the look follows the base building to legendary (base rank 7) and mythic (base rank 10).

### Observatory (follows the Windmill)
- **Hook**: squat and wide, a slit dome with the telescope barrel sticking out. Teal and copper. Never a twin of the windmill's round tower: today, without its sails, the windmill and the observatory look almost the same.
- **Ladder**: rough, a wooden platform with a crude telescope and a star chart · solid, a squat stone drum with a copper dome, its slit shut, a short telescope barrel peeking out, the Sage · grand, the slit wide open, a long brass telescope sticking far out, an armillary sphere on a side terrace (grand must not look like solid: the open dome and the telescope change the silhouette) · legendary, a brass orrery turning around the dome · mythic, **the atlantes**: two stone titans rise at the sides of the drum as pillars and carry the dome, lifted above the drum, on their shoulders; the dome itself becomes the celestial globe, the telescope still pointing out of its slit, banded by the orrery's rings (towering, the shoulders building) [PD, Atlas, telamons], green and blue arcane light, the magic Guillaume asked for: glyph rings drawn in code from the fixed set turn around the globe by day, a beam to the sky and constellations at night, one shaped like the clan crest (layer), the finale. Brass stays matte.
- **Why atlantes**: in the prep's silhouette test, a lone Atlas holding a globe read as a trophy, and the observatory was taken for the Champion Arena. Atlantes are architecture: the silhouette stays "dome on a drum", the observatory's hook, with two figures as columns and rings around it. Never a lone figure holding a ball.
- **References**: the telamons of the temple of Zeus at Agrigento, the atlantes of the New Hermitage in Saint Petersburg, an armillary sphere, a brass orrery.
- **Running gag, the Sage's telescope**: r1 the Sage looks at the sky · r2 the telescope is actually pointed at the tavern · r3 an owl perches on the telescope while the Sage sleeps · legendary, a comet streaks overhead, the Sage falls off his stool · mythic, a small moon mounted on the orrery arm, the Sage waving at it.
- **Build gag**: a star chart flies off in the wind, a worker chasing it.
- **Night**: a beam of light to the sky from legendary on. The Observatory is the star of the night.
- **Never drawn**: any famous modern Atlas statue (for example the one at Rockefeller Center), a lone figure holding a ball.
