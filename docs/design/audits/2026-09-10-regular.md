# UX audit, the regular

Persona: trains nearly every day, three years in, knows their own numbers. Opens this app the
way other people open a training log. Seeded hero, roughly 550 sessions, level 44, English.
Ranked hardest first.

## 1

[shot] 17-journal-stats
[severity] blocker
[claim] My lifetime totals are not my lifetime totals: the stats screen counts 100 workouts while Home counts 547.
[evidence] The tiles read "100 Total Workouts", "2908 Total Minutes", "29 min Avg Duration", and 2908 / 100 = 29.08, so 100 is the divisor. In 18-journal-stats-scrolled the Difficulty Split reads Easy 0, Medium 1, Hard 99, which sums to exactly 100 again. Home's totals row in 02-home-scrolled says 547 Quests. Three years of work, and the one screen that is supposed to add it up is reading a window of 100 rows and calling it "Total".
[fix] Drop the row limit on the stats aggregate query, or if it is deliberate, label every tile and the split with the window it covers ("last 100 workouts"). A number called "Total" that is not the total costs the whole screen its credibility.

## 2

[shot] 21-session-details
[severity] blocker
[claim] I cannot open a past session, so nothing in this app can tell me what I actually did on a given day.
[evidence] The Session Details screen is entirely an error: a dizzy emoji, "Something went wrong", "Session not found", and a Retry button. The row above it in 20-journal-history is consistent with the same break: the newest entry is titled "Quest not found", its duration chip is "--", and only "Medium" survives. The history list is the drill-down path into three years of journal, and the destination does not load.
[fix] Fix the lookup, and make the detail screen degrade instead of erroring: a session whose quest was deleted still has a date, a duration, a difficulty and its logged exercises, and those are the reason I tapped.

## 3

[shot] 18-journal-stats-scrolled
[severity] blocker
[claim] There are no personal records anywhere, so nothing on this screen can ever be beaten.
[evidence] Shots 17, 18 and 19 cover the Stats tab contiguously top to bottom: streak card, three totals tiles, Recent Activity, Workout Days, Difficulty Split, Your Progress, the two trend charts, the calendar. No records card, no best reps, no longest hold, no best session. Meanwhile 20-journal-history stamps a "PR" badge on Chop Wood, so the app does compute records, and there is no screen that names which record that badge is for or what the standing value is.
[fix] Add a records card to Stats, keyed per movement, not the six global session records that stop being beatable in month one: best reps on a movement, longest hold, most volume on a muscle in one session. Then make the history PR badge name the record it broke.

## 4

[shot] 12-exercise-detail
[severity] blocker
[claim] The page for a movement I have done for three years holds none of my numbers on it.
[evidence] Archer Push-Up shows the art, a form description, "No equipment", "tempo 3s/rep", three muscle chips, and a path strip. Not one figure that came from my journal: no best reps, no last result, no number of times performed, no date I last did it. Everything on the page would be identical on a fresh install. The path strip says "RUNG 1 / 5" and "You are on Wall Push-Up" after 550 sessions, and does not say how many clean sessions I am into the rung or how many promote me.
[fix] Two lines under the muscle chips, read off the journal: "Best: N reps, <date>" and "Last time: N reps, <date>". On the path strip, the count toward the next rung ("2 of 3 clean sessions").

## 5

[shot] 18-journal-stats-scrolled
[severity] friction
[claim] "Your Progress" charts whether I showed up, never whether I got better, and the two percentages name no baseline.
[evidence] The card offers Sessions and Minutes, both attendance. The deltas read "Sessions +67%" and "Minutes -16%" with nothing saying against what, so I cannot tell whether that is last week, the same week last month, or my average. The bars below carry no axis, no values and no scale: the tallest Sessions bar and the shortest are visually distinguishable and numerically unknowable. A week of hard singles and a week of easy volume produce the same picture.
[fix] Say the comparison out loud on the delta chips ("vs previous week"), and put the value on top of each bar. Then add a third series that is not attendance: total reps, or reps on the dominant muscle, so the card can show improvement instead of presence.

## 6

[shot] 20-journal-history
[severity] friction
[claim] A history row tells me how long a session lasted and how hard it was set, never what I did in it.
[evidence] Chop Wood, Thu Sep 10, "10 min", "Hard". Tower Climb, "12 min 17s", "Hard". Knight Push, "14 min 34s", "Hard". No reps, no XP, no exercise names, no volume. Two runs of the same quest a month apart are indistinguishable unless one happened to last longer, which is the one thing I am not trying to maximise. The "PR" chip on Chop Wood is the only progress signal in the list and it is unlabelled.
[fix] Add the session's XP and total reps to each row beside the duration chip, and put the beaten record's name in the PR chip.

## 7

[shot] 04-oath
[severity] friction
[claim] Every ready-made goal offered to me is one I passed years ago, and none of them shows where I stand.
[evidence] The list is "50 sessions logged" (Home says 547), "1000 Push-ups in total", "3 sessions a week for 8 weeks", "A 30-day flame" (the journal claims 1092 flame days), "50 leagues beyond the walls". Nothing on any row shows current progress, so I cannot even tell which of these are already banked. The one row that is not a fixed number, "15 x Pull-ups in a row", is also the only one that could be aimed at me, and it is aimed at nobody in particular either.
[fix] Derive the ready-made targets from the journal instead of hardcoding them: the next round number above my current best on each axis, with my current value shown under the row. A goal I have already met is not a goal, it is a receipt.

## 8

[shot] 26-recap-map
[severity] friction
[claim] The one comparison on this screen does not say what it compares to, and the outing is measured against no other outing.
[evidence] "Best league, 4:35 /km" sits under a pace ramp from 8:17 to 4:29. I cannot tell whether that 4:35 is the fastest kilometre inside this run, my best on this route, or my lifetime best, and the three answers mean completely different things. Below it, Ground 5.70 km, Moving 33:02, Pace 5:48 /km, each a bare figure with nothing beside it. The route is named "The Warden's Round", which implies I have run it before, and the screen says nothing about those times.
[fix] Label the scope on that line ("fastest league of this outing" or "your best ever"). Then, since the traces are all on the phone, add one line above the tiles: "Nth time on this round, Mth fastest".

## 9

[shot] 16-village-detail
[severity] friction
[claim] My accumulated effort on a muscle is denominated in a unit that exists nowhere else in the app and can be compared to nothing.
[evidence] Druid Grove: "6915 work units of yoga", "Maximum level reached", then Recent work in the same currency: 60 units, 35 units, 152 units. I have no idea whether 6915 is a lot, what a unit is worth in reps or minutes, how fast I earn them, or what the next threshold would have been had one existed. The five dots are full, so the only readable fact is that this stopped mattering at some unknown point in the past.
[fix] Show the same total in units I logged: reps and minutes. Keep "units" for the dots' internal maths if it must exist, but never as the headline figure on a card whose whole job is to quantify what I did.

## 10

[shot] 01-home-top
[severity] friction
[claim] The level bar tells me I am 0% of the way to something it never names.
[evidence] "Level 44 - Divine 44" over an empty track ending in "0%". No XP figure, no target, no "to level 45". In 34-victory-scrolled the same bar reads 8% after a session, so a whole logged workout is worth eight points of a percentage against an unstated denominator. The lifetime total, "67.2k XP", sits at the bottom of the same screen, far from the bar and in a different unit of account.
[fix] Put the numbers on the bar: "1 240 / 2 000 XP to level 45". The denominator is a constant above level 20 and costs nothing to render.

## 11

[shot] 17-journal-stats
[severity] friction
[claim] The largest number on my stats screen is a tautology and it does not say what keeps it alive.
[evidence] "1092 days, Current streak" with "Best 1092" beside it: the record is the current value, so the only comparison on the card compares the number to itself. Nothing states the quota the flame is measured against, nor how many sessions the current 7-day window holds, which per the design is the thing that actually decides whether tomorrow keeps it. In 19-journal-stats-bottom the same 1092 sits next to "5 Workout days" for September, two numbers two orders of magnitude apart under one calendar with no explanation of why both are true.
[fix] Hide "Best" while it equals current, and replace it with the live figure: "3 of 2 sessions in the last 7 days". That is the number I can act on today.

## 12

[shot] 18-journal-stats-scrolled
[severity] polish
[claim] The Difficulty Split flattens three years into one bar, so the one thing it could prove, that I moved up, is the one thing it cannot show.
[evidence] Easy 0, Medium 1, Hard 99, and a bar that is effectively a single solid pink block with a two-pixel purple sliver at the left edge. It carries no time axis. Whether I have been on Hard for three years or moved there last month reads identically, and the counts are drawn from the same broken 100-row window as finding 1.
[fix] Split the card by period, this month against all time, or plot the mix as a stacked series over the last 12 months. If neither is worth building, the card is a one-line sentence, not a chart.

## 13

[shot] 14-village
[severity] polish
[claim] The village track is finished and nothing on the screen admits it or points anywhere else.
[evidence] "Eternal Capital - Level 44", the top tier, reached at level 40. Every building card carries five filled dots. The only status lines are "Training focus: Shoulders", "Least trained: chest. Its building has stopped rising." and "Nothing happened here today, and that is its own kind of news." So the one thread left is a building that has stopped, and the screen does not say what would restart it or how far off it is.
[fix] When the tier is maxed, say so in the header and give the remaining thread a number: "Chest: 1 240 work units, last raised in June. It rises again at 1 500."

## 14

[shot] 23-settings-top
[severity] polish
[claim] The weekly target that my flame is judged against is not shown or editable where every other preference lives.
[evidence] Preferences holds Village name, Language, Haptics, Countdown beeps, Villagers, Review the guides, Warm-up, Distance unit. Nothing about how many sessions a week I am aiming for, though that quota is what decides whether the 1092 on the previous screen survives the week. Cannot tell from this shot what sits below Distance unit.
[fix] One row: "Weekly target, N sessions", writing the same value an oath sets. It puts the promise and the flame in the same place instead of leaving the promise inside a flow I have to swear my way into.
