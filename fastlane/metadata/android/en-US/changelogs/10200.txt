The boss fight was a progress bar wearing a costume, and the monster was smaller than a push-up.

Now the fight is the screen. The monster fills it, flinches when your set lands, wears its wounds below half health, and goes down when the killing blow does. Every boss has a name now, a voice that answers your hits, and four paintings instead of one.

Health means something. Its pool scales with your difficulty, so easy campaigns finally end in a kill. Empty it with your own damage (beat targets, push past them for critical hits) and the kill is a Triumph worth bonus XP. Fall short and finishing the campaign still fells it: reward for pushing, never punishment.

Beat a boss and it returns legendary: a new form, a bigger name, a bigger pool. One in twenty gleams. Trophies show the fallen monster and its date; tap any painting to see it whole.
