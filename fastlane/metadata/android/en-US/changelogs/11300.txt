- Write your own movements: a name, how to do it, a picture. Edit,
  retire or delete them, and what you wrote leads every list.
- XP counts effort now, not the clock. A long rest no longer pays
  better than training.
- A quest hands you the rung you are on: wall push-ups instead of
  push-ups. Out of reach anyway? Swap it mid-session, or mark the set
  undone instead of typing a 1.
- Pick a backup folder once and Bati writes a snapshot into it before
  every update.
