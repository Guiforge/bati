- Your chronicle can travel at last: Settings → Backup. "Share my
  backup" hands your database to the system share sheet, "Save a file"
  writes it into a folder you choose. One file, your whole year inside,
  encrypted nowhere and sent nowhere.
- "Restore a backup" replaces everything with that file — offered at the
  village gate too, for a new phone. The database you had is kept on the
  device as a recovery copy.
- A file that is not Bati's, damaged, or from a version this one cannot
  read is refused with the reason — never imported.
