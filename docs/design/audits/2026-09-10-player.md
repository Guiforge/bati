# UX audit, the player

One persona, one argument: I came for the game. Ranked hardest first.

---

[shot] 36-boss
[severity] blocker
[claim] The monster is not dangerous, it is a number that has barely moved, printed in the colour the rest of the world uses for "you are fine".
[evidence] A third of the way through the session (33%, round 1/2, exercise 2/3) Nightcoil reads `422 / 425 HP`, and the 422 is green. The full-width hairline at the top edge is still solid across the whole screen. The art is superb and completely inert: a coiled serpent that has lost 0.7% of itself and has no clock, no drain, no counter-pressure, nothing it is doing to me while I do sets to it. Whatever the campaign-level tuning is, the quantity on screen after a real set of Wall Sit is three.
[fix] Stop showing the campaign pool as the live readout. The bar and the numeral should be this session's contribution, "37 of ~140 tonight", with the campaign total demoted to a tick mark. Green is wrong for a hostile creature's health at any value: the doc's own phase palette (red rim, darkening room) is the register, and the HP text should live in it.

---

[shot] 35-boss-narrative
[severity] blocker
[claim] The one moment the world speaks to me is delivered in the shape of an operating system permission prompt.
[evidence] "Walk back down the mountain. The climb only counts if you can still breathe." is genuinely good writing, and it arrives in a rounded card with a solid purple title bar, a filled primary button and a "Not now" text link, floating over a dimmed screen with the forest art greyed out behind it. That is the same widget as "Allow location access". The step's own painting is on screen and is being dimmed to make room for a dialog.
[fix] Kill the card. Full-bleed the beat over the step's art, title in the art's own space, tap anywhere to continue, one button at the bottom. Same words, no chrome.

---

[shot] 36-boss
[severity] blocker
[claim] The screen knows this set is hitting the monster's armour and says nothing, and the fight's only real decision is not on screen at all.
[evidence] Under the name: a target ring on "Abs" and a shield on "Legs". The exercise I am doing right now is Wall Sit, tagged "Legs" on the row directly beneath. So this set is halved, and the only thing the screen tells me is "Keep going! Timer continues after target." Two icons, no words, and nothing connects them to the set in progress. The overshoot-to-crit odds that the design calls the fight's single decision are not printed anywhere in this shot.
[fix] One line under the counter, in words, changing with the exercise: "Legs, resisted, half damage" or "Abs, weak point, x1.5" or the live crit chance once past target. The information is already computed; it is being spent on two unlabelled glyphs at the top instead of on the moment I am deciding whether to hold ten more seconds.

[shot] 26-recap-map
[severity] blocker
[claim] I just came back from an expedition and the app paid me in Strava's currency, not its own. Nothing in the world moved.
[evidence] The trace itself is the best drawing in the app: pace-graded, glowing, no basemap needed. Everything under it belongs to another product. `GROUND 5.70 km · MOVING 33:02 · PACE 5:48 /km` in flat grey capitals, "Best league, 4:35 /km" as a caption, and then the largest block of text on the screen is a four-line paragraph about OpenFreeMap tile servers. Leagues are supposed to be the one currency nothing else pays, the thing that raises the road out of the village. The word appears once, as a unit on a pace figure. The village is not mentioned. XP is not mentioned.
[fix] Put the world above the fold: the leagues earned and what they moved ("the road reaches the second waymark"), then the numbers. Move the tile-server paragraph behind the "Show the map" button, where it is actually the decision being made.

[shot] 14-village, 15-village-scrolled
[severity] friction
[claim] The village's picture is finished and its progress is not, so from here on every session I train is paid in purple dots.
[evidence] "Eternal Capital, Level 44" is the top of twelve tiers: the painting overhead can never change again. Below it, seventeen tiles in a three-column grid, each an icon, a name and five dots. Watchtower, Castle Wall, Armory, Fountain, Observatory and Barn still sit at three of five, so there is real progress left, and none of it is visible in the scene it is supposed to be building. The painting and the grid are two separate progress systems that never look at each other, and the design says there is only one. This is also the exact thing the anti-references rule out: the reward for training is a list of things with meters on them.
[fix] The remaining building levels must land on the painting, not beside it. Milestone reveals on the scene are already the stated mechanic; wire the six unfinished buildings to visible details in the Eternal Capital art and let the grid become the explanation of a change I already saw.

[shot] 33-victory
[severity] friction
[claim] I finished a session and the loudest thing on the screen is a warning triangle in an amber alert box. The session itself is a rounded total.
[evidence] Cannot tell from the shots whether a victory screen preceded this. What this shot shows is the first screen after training: a brown-bordered card headed by a caution triangle, "Resume workout? You have an unfinished session", sitting above everything. The only trace of the work done is in the header: the XP total went from `67.0k` to `67.2k` and the level bar from 0% to 8%. Two hundred XP rounded away to nothing by its own formatting, and a percent.
[fix] The resume card must never outrank the session that just ended, and it should not be styled as an error: an interrupted workout is an invitation, not a fault. Drop the triangle and the amber, and show the session's actual XP as a delta on the bar ("+204") rather than folding it into a k-rounded lifetime total.

[shot] 05-adventures
[severity] friction
[claim] Three years in, level 44, 547 quests logged, and the world offers me the tutorial first.
[evidence] The top card is "The Squire's Path": five empty progress dashes, "Every hero begins as the one who carries the shield", "up to +63 XP per step" for a hero who earns roughly three times that in one session. Both visible cards are tagged "ROUTE". Nothing on this screen is aware that I have played the game, and nothing tells me which of these has a monster worth the trip.
[fix] Sort by threat rather than catalogue order, surface the boss campaigns above the routes, and put the threat rank on the card the way the boss panel already does. A level 44 hero's first card should be the hardest unstarted thing in the catalogue.

---

[shot] 07-adventure-steps
[severity] friction
[claim] A five-chapter campaign is presented as a settings list, and the same screen proves it did not have to be.
[evidence] Four rows, each an 80px thumbnail, a truncated line of story, the word "Locked" and the sentence "Finish the previous step to unlock it" repeated verbatim on every one. The cover art directly above gets 450px of the same screen. The steps have their own paintings, a ruined shield, a pine trail, a coiled thing, a ploughed field, and they are being shown at the size of a favicon.
[fix] One road down the screen, steps as stations on it, art at a size a person can read, position doing the work the repeated "Finish the previous step" sentence is doing now. Keep the list only for the finished ones.

[shot] 29-session-active, 28-session-warmup
[severity] friction
[claim] The screen I spend ninety percent of my time on is the one screen that stops looking like this game.
[evidence] A grinning woman mid star jump on a flat grey studio backdrop with pink and cyan sparkles; a smiling man in a wall sit over an olive-khaki wash. Both are brightly lit, cheerful, and drawn in a different hand from the axe in the pines, from Nightcoil, from the Eternal Capital. On 29 the whole lower half of the screen carries the plate's khaki tint, so the palette leaves dark fantasy entirely for the duration of the set.
[fix] Needs a redesign of the exercise plates: same inked, unlit, dark-fantasy register as every other painting, and no smiling. Cheap interim: drop the ambient colour sampling that turns the session screen khaki, and darken the plate ground so the figure is lit rather than the room.

---

[shot] 09-quest-detail
[severity] friction
[claim] A villager is standing on the two controls the screen exists for.
[evidence] The blacksmith's body covers the "Easy" pill and the left third of "Start Quest"; his speech bubble covers "Medium", "Hard" and the targets line. At this instant the primary action of the screen is partly behind a man. Cannot tell from a still how long he stays, and a breath would be fine, but a still is also what a player sees when they look down mid-decision.
[fix] Reserve his lane. Cameos enter in the empty band under the CTA or over the art, never over a control. If there is no empty band on a screen, that screen gets no cameo.

---

[shot] 02-home-scrolled
[severity] polish
[claim] Three years of training are summarised as two numbers that can never be beaten.
[evidence] At the bottom of Home: crossed swords, "547 Quests", a trophy, "67.0k XP". Both are lifetime totals, both only ever tick up by a fraction of a percent, and neither can produce a notable result on any given day. They are the only place Home acknowledges history at all.
[fix] Replace them with something this week can win: the best single thing this session or this week was, phrased as a superlative. The journal already holds enough to find one every time.

---

[shot] 04-oath
[severity] polish
[claim] Swearing an oath is the most ceremonial thing the app asks of me, and it is served as a settings menu.
[evidence] Eight identical pills, the same purple star icon eight times, the same chevron eight times, in the same weight and colour. "A 30-day flame", "1000 x Push-ups in total", "50 leagues beyond the walls" and "30 s L-Sit hold" are four completely different kinds of promise flattened into one row shape. Header: "READY-MADE OATHS".
[fix] No new art needed. Group them by what they promise, consistency, volume, skill, distance, and give the chosen one a single sentence of ceremony on the confirm step. An oath that costs one tap and no words is not an oath.

[shot] 16-village-detail
[severity] polish
[claim] The sheet's headline number is in a unit that exists nowhere else and that I cannot convert into anything.
[evidence] "6915 work units of yoga", then "Maximum level reached", then a "Recent work" ledger in the same unit: 60 units, 35 units, 152 units. Nothing tells me what a work unit is, whether 6915 is a lot, or what it bought.
[fix] Say it in something I logged: minutes trained, or reps. Keep the ledger, change the unit.
