- One tap on Home and you are out: walk, run or ride, with no
  setting and no countdown. The clock answers the only question a
  departure has, how long did I keep going.
- Your phone can sleep in your pocket. The screen goes dark,
  Android locks it, and the notification runs the outing, elapsed
  time first.
- Finishing takes a held thumb, or the notification's own action
  from the lock screen. A mistap undoes in one gesture.
- Every league you cross is a buzz.
- Setting up an outing takes four taps: the durations and
  distances people actually name, half marathon included.
- On the recap of a free outing, you can turn it into a quest and
  run it again.
- A two hour outing is finally worth two hours, and an outing your
  phone interrupted is worth what its trace proves.
