- Three ways out: walk, run or ride. Your phone measures the
  ground, offline, and the route is drawn when you get back.
- Leagues build the High Road in your village, and an oath can
  be sworn on them.
- The Journal totals the ground you have covered and remembers
  your longest outing.
- One notification, naming the quest, for the whole walk. The map is
  a setting, off by default: on, a map host learns roughly where
  you went.
- The Watcher has opinions about the road now.
