# UX audit, the stranger

Persona: never lifted, never played an RPG, installed this because a friend said it makes
training less boring. Thirty seconds of patience. Fresh install, English, day one.
Twelve screenshots, onboarding then every tab.

---

[shot] 48-quests-day-one
[severity] blocker
[claim] I was asked what my level was, then handed 37 workouts with no level on any of them, so I cannot pick one.
[evidence] Onboarding said "What's your level? So we can suggest the right workouts". This screen says "37 quests" in a pink badge and "Single workouts. Pick one and go", and then nothing on it knows I answered. The filters are Duration, Type, Muscles, Equipment: no Level, no Beginner. "Chop Wood" reads "Circuit - Shoulders - Legs - Arms, 3 exercises, up to +140 XP" and "Tower Climb" reads "Muscle - Back - Shoulders - Arms": the only difference I can read is which body parts, which is exactly the thing I do not know how to choose between. Nothing says "start here". The eye goes to "37", which is the number of decisions I now owe.
[fix] Put the answer to the level question on the list: a "For you" or "Beginner" row pinned at the top, or a difficulty word on each card next to the minutes. If the level answer cannot reach this screen, stop asking for it in onboarding.

[shot] 45-home-day-one
[severity] blocker
[claim] Onboarding offered me an eight minute first workout, then home forgot it and the one big button sends me to a catalogue instead.
[evidence] The card is titled "Start your journey", labelled "Quick Workout" in yellow, and the button says "Pick a quest". Those are three different promises in one card, and there is a large empty band between the yellow label and the button where the name of the workout should be. "Your first march" from the last onboarding step appears nowhere on this screen. So the one thing I had already agreed to do is gone, and the replacement asks me to shop.
[fix] Put the first session back in that card: title it with the actual workout, keep the minutes and the movement count from the onboarding step, and make the button "Start, 8 min". Reserve "Pick a quest" for a secondary link.

[shot] 47-adventures-day-one
[severity] blocker
[claim] I cannot tell what this tab is for or how it differs from Quests, and every word on the card is one I would have to be taught.
[evidence] The subtitle "Multi-workout programs with a story and a boss fight" is the only plain sentence here and its last two words run underneath the settings gear, so what I actually read is "Multi-workout programs with a story and a boss". The card then says "ROUTE" in caps, "The Squire's Path", "Circuit - Abs - Legs - Back", a description cut off at "Five marches to e...", then "~ 2 weeks - 5 steps" and "up to +63 XP per step". Route, circuit, march, step, path, quest, boss: six words for things I have no map of, and a two week commitment attached. Nothing on the card tells me how long one session is or what I would do in it.
[fix] Move the subtitle clear of the gear, and put on each card the two facts I can act on: minutes per session and sessions per week. Cut "ROUTE" or spell it in the same words as the subtitle.

[shot] 43-onboarding-level
[severity] friction
[claim] The three things I am supposed to press look less pressable than the greyed out text below them, and one of the three labels does not mean anything to me.
[evidence] "Beginner", "Regular" and "Advanced" are dark pills with a thin outline on a dark forest photo. Below them "Start my training journey" is bold, larger, centred and grey, which is what a broken button looks like, and below that "Skip" is bright blue and the most clickable thing on the screen. Nothing is preselected. "Regular" is the middle option and I cannot tell if it means I train regularly or that I am average: I have never trained at all, so neither reading helps me place myself.
[fix] Give each option one line of what it means in plain terms, for example "Beginner, new to training or coming back". Make the selected option obviously filled, and hide or dim the primary until it is real rather than showing a bold grey word.

[shot] 41-onboarding-village-empty
[severity] friction
[claim] The button at the bottom is the same sentence I already pressed on the previous screen, and it is grey, so I think the app has stalled.
[evidence] Screen one had a purple "Start my training adventure". Screen two ends with the identical words in flat grey with no pill behind them. The only hint is "3 chars min" in small text on the far left, under the field, on the opposite side from where my thumb and my eye are. Pressing that button after typing does not start training either, it goes to another question.
[fix] Label the step button for the step: "Next". Keep the adventure line for the button that genuinely starts a workout, and move "3 chars min" into the field placeholder.

[shot] 44-onboarding-first-session
[severity] friction
[claim] This is the clearest screen in the app and it still will not tell me which four movements I am about to do.
[evidence] "Eight minutes, four movements, no equipment. A squire has to start somewhere." Minutes, count and kit are exactly what I needed. Then two thirds of the screen is a picture of a sea, and "Start now" asks me to begin an exercise session where I do not know if the four movements are push ups or something I physically cannot do. There is no "see what's in it".
[fix] List the four movement names under the subtitle, in the empty band that is already there. Four short lines cost nothing and remove the only reason I would press "Later".

[shot] 49-village-day-one
[severity] friction
[claim] Three things have been built for me, each has a row of dots and a bar, and nothing on the screen says what the dots count or what fills them.
[evidence] "Campfire", "Tent" and "Training Dummy" each show one filled dot out of five and a bar about a quarter full. One filled dot with a quarter bar is two different progress readings on the same card, and neither has a number, a unit or a label. Above them the section is called "Built" with no explanation, and I never built anything. The only instruction, "Every muscle you train raises a building. Tap one to see what feeds it", sits under "To build", so it reads as being about the four dim shapes below, not about the three cards above.
[fix] Put the sentence "Every muscle you train raises a building" above "Built" where the built cards are, and label one card's progress with words: "Level 1 of 5, 3 more leg workouts".

[shot] 45-home-day-one
[severity] friction
[claim] A row of three cards with play buttons on them, under a heading that tells me nothing, and I cannot tell what pressing play commits me to.
[evidence] The heading is "Head out" with "Set up" on the right. The cards are "Warden's Walk", "Messenger's Run", "Outrider's Ride", each with a play triangle in a circle, the universal sign for "this starts immediately". No duration, no distance, no line saying these happen outdoors. The word walk, run and ride are inside the fantasy names, which is the only clue, and the third card is half off screen.
[fix] One line under "Head out": "Walk, run or ride. Start when you leave, stop when you're back." Put the play icon behind a tap on the card rather than on the card face, so nothing looks armed.

[shot] 47-adventures-day-one
[severity] friction
[claim] A character has appeared over the second card and covers it completely, with nothing on her that I can press to make her go.
[evidence] A speech box reading "Adventures chain quests together and end on a boss. You can stop between two. It will wait." sits over the whole right half of the second card. Underneath it I can see "The Lumber Route" and a line of purple words cut off at "Circuit - Legs - Shoulders - Abs". There is no cross, no "got it", no dimmed backdrop that suggests tapping outside works. The sentence is genuinely the most useful one on the screen, and it is stapled over content.
[fix] Move that sentence into the header where the subtitle already is, or show the cameo in the gap below the last card. If it must overlay, give it a visible dismiss.

[shot] 43-onboarding-level
[severity] friction
[claim] Four screens in a row and not one of them has a way back, so a wrong tap is permanent as far as I can tell.
[evidence] The dot indicator at the top says I am on step three of four. The top left corner is empty on every onboarding screen, top right is the settings gear. There is no arrow, no "Back". If I mistype my village name on step two and notice on step three, nothing on screen offers to return.
[fix] Add a back arrow top left on steps two through four. It is the corner that is already empty.

[shot] 45-home-day-one
[severity] friction
[claim] A bar pinned to the bottom half covers a row of numbers, and it calls my village a word I never typed.
[evidence] Above the pinned strip there is a sliver of a cut off row: a partial yellow circle and the tops of some characters. Scrolling reveals it was "0 Quests, 0 XP", so on first load part of the page is permanently hidden behind the strip. The strip itself reads "Hamlet, Level 1" with a chevron. I named the place Testville, and the Village tab agrees, showing "Testville, Hamlet - Level 1". On home the name is gone and only the word I did not choose is left.
[fix] Add bottom padding equal to the strip height so nothing sits under it, and show the village name on the strip with Hamlet as the smaller second line, the same order the Village tab uses.

[shot] 40-onboarding-presentation
[severity] polish
[claim] The only other thing on the first screen offers me a backup, on an app I installed sixty seconds ago.
[evidence] Under the purple button the underlined link reads "I already have a backup". On a phone where this app has never existed, that sentence describes a state I cannot be in, and "backup" is a word about files rather than about training. It is the only alternative offered, so I read it looking for an escape and find a dead end.
[fix] "Restore from a previous phone", and it can sit further down or behind a smaller link. The word restore says what happens, backup names a thing I do not have.

[shot] 50-journal-day-one-stats
[severity] polish
[claim] Two different views of this tab look identical, and the picture on it is from a different app.
[evidence] The stats view and the history view show the same title "Quest Journal", the same "No tales yet", the same "Complete quests to fill your journal" and the same "Browse quests" button, with no tab, segment or label anywhere naming which one I am looking at. Cannot tell from the shots what switched them. The illustration is a bright cartoon scroll emoji, sitting in an app whose every other image is inked and dark, including the woman in the corner of the same screen.
[fix] Show the two views' names as a segmented control under the title so they are visibly two things, and replace the emoji with an art asset from the same set as the rest of the app.

[shot] 45-home-day-one
[severity] polish
[claim] There are two gear icons in the top bar and I do not know which one is settings.
[evidence] The avatar at top left carries a small gear badge on its lower right corner. The circular button at top right is also a gear, ringed with a flame. Same shape, same grey, twelve centimetres apart, and nothing labels either.
[fix] Keep one gear. Whatever the flamed one does, give it its own shape.
