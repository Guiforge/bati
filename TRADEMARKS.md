## What you can do

- Fork, build, modify and redistribute under the licences above.
- Say that your project is a fork of, or based on, Bati, and link to this repository.
- Use the name in plain text to describe compatibility ("opens Bati backup files").
- Use screenshots of the app in reviews, articles and tutorials.

## What a fork must change

- **The name and the icon**: pick your own, and do not present the build as the official one.
- **The package name**: anything installable next to or instead of the official app needs its own
  application id, or it will replace the official one on a phone, or fail to install over it.
- **The signing key**: sign with your own.
- **The contact, policy and update links**: the privacy policy, the support address and the
  version check point at this project and describe its behaviour, not yours.

If you are unsure, write to **contact@getbati.app** before you publish.
